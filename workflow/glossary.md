# Ocarina of Time: Croatian glossary (hrvatski pojmovnik)

Use these translations every time the English term appears. Decline them normally (padeži): "Kokirijeva šuma" → "u Kokirijevoj šumi", "Gospodarski mač" → "Gospodarskim mačem".

## Rules
- Proper names of people, races and creatures stay as in English but decline in Croatian: Link → Linka, Linku, Linkom; Zelda → Zelde, Zeldi; Ganondorf → Ganondorfa; Navi → Navi (does not decline); Saria → Sarije, Sariji; Ruto → Ruto; Impa → Impe; Malon → Malon; Darunia → Darunia (nom.), Darunije (gen.), Daruniji (dat.), Daruniju (acc.), Darunijom (instr.), "Darunia je rekao" (never "Darunije" as the subject); Epona → Epone; Dampé → Dampéa; Mido → Mida.
- Keep race names: Kokiri (jedan Kokiri, Kokiriji), Goron (Goroni), Zora (Zore), Gerudo (Gerudo, pl. Gerudo), Sheikah (Sheikah), Hylian → Hilijac / hilijski, Deku (adjective: Deku), Hyrule → Hyrule (u Hyruleu, Hyrulea).
- Address the player with "ti" (informal). Link is male (masculine verb forms: "otišao si", "pronašao si").
- Rupees: rupija (1 rupija, 2–4 rupije, 5+ rupija).
- "Hey! Listen!" (Navi) → "Hej! Slušaj!"

## Gender of characters (rod)
Use the right gender in verbs and adjectives, both when the character speaks about themself and when others speak about them.
- **Feminine:** Navi (vila: "rekla sam", "sigurna sam", "Navi se trgnula"), Zelda, Impa, Saria, Ruto, Malon, Nabooru, Twinrova, Koume, Kotake, the Great Fairies, Epona, Anju, Cucco lady.
- **Masculine:** Link, Sheik (until the reveal Sheik is a man: "Sheik je rekao", "bio sam"; after the reveal the speaker is Zelda, feminine), Rauru, Darunia, Ganondorf, Kaepora Gaebora, Mido, King Zora, the Great Deku Tree, Talon, Ingo, Dampé.
